# Related work and audit boundaries

This is a bounded audit of directly relevant upstream work, refreshed on 2026-11-17. Issue bodies and available comments were inspected; for the three pull requests, review comments, reviews, and changed-file material were also fetched. [Status metadata](upstream-status.json) records exact lookup time and commits. Full responses and the earlier research audit remain in the original workspace. Linked follow-ups named below were not all re-audited, so their current implementation status is unknown here.

An upstream performance number applies to its own task, inputs, output, hardware, code, and measurement protocol. None of the upstream throughput gains or proposed root causes is adopted as a local A6000 result. Merged does not mean every workload is solved; Open does not mean the work is absent.

## Local inclusion checks

The pinned `models/minicpm_o/config.py` selects preprocessing concurrency four. `components/preprocessor.py` uses request-local connector scope, and `stages.py` selects `ThreadedSimpleScheduler`. The cross-request audio batching module from #2320 is absent. The two-file #3701 patch explicitly constructs `ThinkerRequest` and restores `omni_model_inputs` and the embedding cursor on retraction. These are source comparisons, not conclusions drawn from commit dates or package versions.

Existing measurements of length, padding, batching, and KV bookkeeping establish local baseline behavior. Their novelty is not the mechanism itself. An Exploratory extension would require a precise unresolved question and evidence beyond the reviewed mechanisms. The repository makes no new confirmed bottleneck claim merely by collecting these references.

Official background: [model card](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-4_5), [reference demo](https://github.com/OpenBMB/MiniCPM-o-Demo/tree/main/MiniCPMO45), [runtime architecture](https://sgl-project.github.io/sglang-omni/developer_reference/main.html). These current pages explain concepts; they are not installation or launch instructions for the frozen environment.
